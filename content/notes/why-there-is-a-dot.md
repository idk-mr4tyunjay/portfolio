The dot after my name on the home page is a full stop, a cursor and a status light at the same time.

It comes from S.H. Raza's bindu paintings: one black point on a ground, which he described as the seed of everything and also nothing at all. I wanted one mark that could carry the whole site, so every line on the page grows out of that point and gathers back into it at the bottom.

It also does a job. At build time a script fetches every project URL on the page and asks GitHub for my latest public commit. If every link answers, the dot is blue. If any of them fail, it stays black and the receipt at the bottom says how many answered and when. Nothing is checked in the browser; the result is baked into the HTML.

The tree is a banyan. Products are the canopy, the servers and the notes about what broke are the roots. The leaves are the only thing that moves on its own, and there are never more than four in the air.

The figure sitting under the roots with a cup is drawn in the Warli style. If you know the comic it is quoting, you know how most of these notes started.
